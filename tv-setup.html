Checking your TV browser…

What do you see on the TV's Home screen?

Install the VELORA Android TV app

Choose Download VELORA TV. The television may then ask permission to install downloaded apps and require you to confirm the installation.

Download VELORA TV

This works only on compatible Android-based TVs or Fire TV devices that permit APK installation. You must approve the TV's security prompts; Velora cannot skip them. If nothing downloads or the TV says the file is unsupported, use the browser option or ask for help identifying its operating system.

Watch TV without installing

Your TV may not allow website app installation.

JVC and other brands use several different operating systems. Some only allow apps from their own store and cannot install an Android APK or create a home-screen icon from a website.

For now: use the large button below to open the remote-friendly VELORA player. If your browser supports setting a startup page, this address can be used there, but Velora cannot change the TV browser's startup page itself.

Open VELORA Live TV

If this TV cannot pin websites or install the VELORA APK, the dependable way to get a dedicated app icon is to use a compatible Android TV or Google TV streaming device connected by HDMI.

Identify the TV in under a minute

  1. Press Home on the TV remote.
  2. Look for Google Play Store, Google TV, Android TV, Fire TV, Roku, VIDAA or another app store.
  3. If it's unclear, open Settings → About / Device Information and photograph the model name. No password or private account details are needed.

The JVC TruSound label describes a TV feature; it does not identify the operating system.

Open VELORA for now

Already have an Android TV app installed?

Press Home → Apps → VELORA. Once installed, you should not need to type the web address every time.
